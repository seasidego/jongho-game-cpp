## Cards
- CardName(cost) - Ability and Unique Ability

### Treasure
- Copper(0) - 1 coin
- Silver(3) - 2 coin
- Gold(6) - 3 coin

### Victory
- Estate(2) - 1 score
- Duchy(5) - 3 score
- Province(8) - 6 score

### Action(Unique Ability)
- Chapel(2) - Trash up to 4 cards from your hand.
- Cellar(2) - 1 action, Discard any number of cards. +1 Card per card discarded
- Moneylender(4) - You may trash a Copper from your hand for +3 coin
- Workshop(3) - Gain a card costing up to 4
- Merchant(3) - 1 card, 1 action, The first time you play a Silver this turn. +1 coin

### Action(Ability)
- Village(3) - 1 card, 2 action
- Market(5) - 1 card, 1 action, 1 coin, 1 buy
- Laboratory(5) - 2 cards, 1 action
- Festival(5) - 2 action, 1 buy, 2 coin
- Smithy(4) - 3 cards
